# RunStreak

**From couch to consistent**

> #24 of 33 · Wave 2: Recurring keepers · Used: 3 times a week · Harder · Revenue potential: Medium · Habits, Health & Learning

## What it is

A beginner-friendly run and walk-run tracker: GPS distance and pace, run/walk intervals, a weekly goal and streaks, simple plans and shareable run cards.

## Why it's in the portfolio

Fitness habits retain well while people are training, and run cards are very shareable. But it's competitive (Strava, and the free NHS Couch to 5K app), so it must be the simplest, kindest option for beginners.

## Who it's for

New runners, casual joggers, people training for a first 5K.

**The problem:** Beginners get overwhelmed by serious running apps and quit.

## Version one

- GPS tracking: distance, pace, time, route
- Run/walk intervals with audio cues
- Weekly goal and streaks
- Simple beginner plans
- Shareable run cards

**Example:** A beginner follows a 5K plan, logs three runs a week, and shares a card after week four.

## Later

- Plans beyond 5K
- Run with a friend
- Offline maps

(streaks with forgiveness, visible progress, milestones, recaps, no guilt-trips).

| Stage | In RunStreak |
|---|---|
| Cue | Planned run days |
| Tiny win | Complete a run |
| Progress | Weekly streak and plan progress |
| Share | Run cards |
| Next step | The next plan after 5K |
| Cadence | 3 times a week |

## Animation and tone

**Playful.** Celebrations, confetti, cute characters, satisfying sounds and mild comic reactions. Nothing tragic.

- Finish-line burst and a badge after a run

**Line we don't cross:** Nothing animated during the run except the essentials.

## AI

**None.** Not needed. Plain code does everything this app does.

## Watch-outs

Background GPS, battery use and accuracy are hard to get right. Strong free competition.
